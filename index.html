Sound familiar?

A few things keep families off the trail. A guided Wander hike takes all of them off your plate.

Screens are winning

Kids average 7+ hours on devices a day. The trail is right there — but so is the phone. A hike they love is the best screen-off hours you'll get all week.

"Hiking is boring"

On most hikes, adults hike and kids endure. Ours are built the other way around — the trail itself becomes a game, so kids are pulling you forward instead of asking to go home.

Where do we even go?

Researching a kid-friendly hike eats an evening. Too hard? Parking? Safe for a four-year-old? We've already picked the trail, checked the conditions, and planned the day.

The bar feels too high

"What if we're not prepared?" keeps families home. We send everything ahead — what to wear, what to bring, how to prep the night before — so there's no excuse left.

A great family hike shouldn't take a planning committee. Show up, meet your guide, and let the kids lead the way. That's the whole idea.

How a Wander hike works

From booking to the drive home, every step is designed so your family arrives ready and leaves wanting more.

1

Book your spot

Pick a hike and a date. You'll get a confirmation, a short parent guide, and — for the kids — a fun video to get them excited for the adventure.

2

Get ready together

Watch the kids' video, lay out clothes, and pick your child's trail snack pack online. We'll have it packed and waiting at the trailhead.

3

Meet your guide

No planning, no navigating, no worrying about the pace. Your guide knows the trail, carries the first-aid kit, and keeps the whole thing fun and safe.

4

Kids earn their way down the trail

Along the route, kids earn Wander bucks through challenges and discoveries — turning "are we there yet?" into "what's next?"

5

Finish with a reward

At the end, kids spend their Wander bucks on snacks, small gear, or a discount on the next hike. Summits deserve more than just a photo.

Built for kids, not just tolerated by them

The trail becomes as engaging as anything on a screen — with real-world adventure your guide brings to every hike.

Wander bucks

Kids earn play money on the trail and spend it at the finish — including toward their next adventure.

Choose-your-own snack pack

Kids pick their trail snacks online before the hike. Half the fun starts before you leave the house.

Nature discovery

Spot, name, and learn the plants, animals, and tracks along the way — facts kids actually remember.

Badges & challenges

Complete goals to earn badges and climb the family leaderboard. (The kids always beat the parents.)

Trail stories & treasure

Key spots along the route each come with a story — and sometimes a hidden treasure waiting to be found.

Kid-paced routes

Every trail is chosen for the right length and payoff — a lake, a waterfall, a view — with turnaround points if little legs give out.

Wander started as a simple question — what if the hike itself was the thing kids couldn't wait to do again? So I started guiding the kind of family hikes I always wished existed: planned down to the snacks, paced for little legs, and built so kids are the point, not passengers.
